How the final assessment runs Qualified

As the Guideline sets it out, so that a reader can see what this portfolio is built for. The file I hold, dated 25 September 2026, still carries tracked changes and editorial comments. I therefore treat it as a working version and not yet as the procedure in force; the PD Officer is to confirm the governing text.

Before
The supervisory team concludes that the portfolio shows sufficient achievement of all learning goals and verifies that every required component is present. The Graduate Committee Arts & Creative then makes a formal completeness check, which is not a judgement of quality.
Committee
Appointed on the supervisory team’s motivated proposal. The Guideline names an independent chair who is a lector, two lectoren independent of the trajectory, and two external professionals; members of the supervisory team cannot serve. The Committee Proposal Form is worded differently: a minimum of two lectoren and two professionals, a maximum of six members, and the independent chair proposed separately from the Graduate Committee, as a top three. Whether the chair counts among the lectoren and within the six is to be confirmed.
Portfolio review
Members read the portfolio independently, using the PD Arts + Creative Assessment Framework, and then discuss it together. Within six weeks the committee decides whether the candidate may proceed to the examination. The framework is applied holistically, not as a checklist or a scoring instrument.
Oral examination
The formal examination: a conversation with the committee on the portfolio, the research trajectory, the artistic and creative work and the learning goals, in a semi-closed or closed session. The candidate sets the format with the supervisory team and the chair. The qualification is “Good” or “Groundbreaking”.
Public presentation
Organised by the candidate for a wider audience, in a form suited to the project. It is not an assessment moment and is not part of the committee’s evaluation. It may take place before, after or separately from the oral examination, provided both are completed within the final assessment procedure.
Archiving
The programme archives an online portfolio in the SAR Research Catalogue at the end of the trajectory. A portfolio held elsewhere, such as this site, is linked to or deposited there after the assessment. It should be a coherent, navigable whole.

During the pilot phase the Professional Doctorate cannot yet be formally conferred. Accreditation and legal recognition of the title are expected in 2028, and candidates who complete the pilot successfully before then are intended to receive the degree retrospectively.
