Which rate turns hours into EC

The Graduate Network’s individual-training form divides hours by 24. An earlier version of this page used 24 without saying so. Both are shown below, and I am asking the Graduate Network which applies to individual elements. Until it answers, the individual total is given as a range, not as a figure.

  • 2023: 1 EC, Discursive Context: AI in Artistic Practice (June).

  • 2025: 2 EC, the third iteration, with a baseline questionnaire at the first class (N = 10).
