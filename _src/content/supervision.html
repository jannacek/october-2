Supervision

Who supervises, what was agreed in the Training and Supervision Plan, what the record shows, and where supervision changed the work.

Overview in place Reflection to be written 27 Oct 2026 · all four supervisors, Groningen

The supervisory team was assembled around the questions of this trajectory: artistic research in a social context, digital sovereignty and ethics, health technology, and AI theory with its institutional networks. This page sets out the agreement, the record that exists, and the moments where supervision visibly shaped the work.

Team
Two supervising professors and two supervising professionals, for the length of the trajectory (four years or part-time equivalent).
Time
30–40 person-days a year across the team in the TSP: 20 for the first supervisor, 5 for each of the others.
Entitlements
At least one supervisory meeting every fortnight with one or more team members, and at least three meetings a year with the whole team.
Candidate time
75% research, 12.5% teaching, 12.5% training and education.
Principles
The Leiden Golden Rules for supervision, as subscribed to in the TSP.

Where supervision shaped the work Updated

Three turns in the trajectory are visible in the record. In each, the direction came out of the dialogue, not from the candidate alone.

2023–24

From a wide frame to a literacy question

The original proposal carried a broad title, Lifestyle and Digital Sovereignty, and a holistic-care frame. After AT-LAB I and the first evaluation, the question narrowed to AI and data literacy. This was a sharpening, not a change of route, and it made the following labs more precise.

2024

Making the process visible

The June 2024 feedback asked for the process to be shared and made visible, and for students to be involved. The September 2024 student showcase, Encounters in Artistic Research, and this portfolio site both follow that line.

November 2025

From new outputs to consolidation

The full team asked for synthesis: say what an AT-LAB is, how the labs iterate, and how the cases answer the research questions. The 2026 work picks these requests up: the definition of the format, the three lenses, the secondary analysis of AT-LAB I and the methods manuscript. See the 2026 consolidation.

Between these turns, the status of ATMOS_FERA moved from an artistic project run alongside the PD to AT-LAB IV, a lab with a question, an enacted intervention, a reading and a feed-forward. The case is made on its own page.
