Since the March 2026 version

Updates since March

What is new in the work, what I corrected in earlier wording, how the portfolio is organised now, and what is still open.

Four-lab structure unchanged Claims recalibrated to the evidence Open items listed below

The structure of the trajectory has not changed: four AT-LABs, one format, three lenses. What changed since March is how much I can now say about them, and how carefully each statement is sized to its evidence.

New work

Consolidation

The AT-LAB format is now written as a methods manuscript, the three lenses are named and applied, AT-LAB I has an auditable secondary analysis, AT-LAB III has an analytic autoethnography, and ATMOS_FERA has a peer-reviewed paper.
Sharper claims

Three levels of claim

Every lab and contribution separates what is demonstrated, what is designed for, and what is open. Outputs carry their current status: presented, accepted, in review, in preparation.
Assessment

Easier to assess

An assessment map sets each of the five required portfolio components and each learning goal against the evidence, with a readiness panel. The training, supervision, dissemination and evidence pages state what is missing.

Claims I corrected or recalibrated

Several statements in the March version went further than the evidence, or were simply wrong. They are changed throughout, and listed here so that anyone who saw the earlier version can see what moved.

AT-LAB I: what changed in participants “Conceptual clarity was low at entry, substantially higher post-experience” Removed. There was no before-and-after measure. What the 27 forms show is how people articulated the term “digital sovereignty”: 3 defined it, 2 partly, 18 did not know it.

How the site changed

  • Evidence first. Each lab page now states what its evidence is and what it cannot support, and ends in the same three-part claim: demonstrated, designed for, open.
  • New pages and sections. This page; the assessment map with the five learning goals and a readiness panel; a 2026 consolidation on the trajectory; a dissemination ledger; an EC meter on the training page; the supervision record.
  • A clearer visual system. One geometric motif per lab, in the lab’s own colour; text colours chosen for AA contrast; a layout that works on a phone; and a print stylesheet for the committee.
  • Accessible by default. Skip link, keyboard-navigable sidebar, reduced-motion respected, and no content hidden behind scripts.
