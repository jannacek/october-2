De Papieren Machine, Koninklijke Bibliotheek New

A workshop in which visitors build a very small next-word model by hand, out of old Dutch newspapers. It was selected through the open call of the KB and AIxDESIGN for the exhibition Broncode: boeken in het AI-tijdperk. It is upcoming: it has not been delivered yet, and nothing here reports a result.

When and where
Thursday 29 October 2026, evening, at the KB, The Hague, during Festival Broncode: taal in het AI-tijdperk (festival programme 18:30–22:00), which opens the exhibition. KB programme page ↗
Who
The exhibition and festival are the KB’s, with AIxDESIGN as co-curator. AIxDESIGN ↗
My part
An artist talk on my practice and my view of AI, and a drop-in workshop booth during the evening. The longer workshop of 2 h 30, from which the booth version is drawn, is fully designed and documented in the print pack.
Status
Upcoming Documentation of the day, and of the two paper archives it leaves behind, will be added after the event.

What participants do

They act as the machine. From facsimiles of Dutch newspapers, 1618–1879, digitised by the KB and published on Delpher, they decide what goes into a corpus, how to cut the text into words, and how the next word is drawn by frequency. A fourth operation, classification, runs beside the model and never feeds it, to show that the two are often confused.

What it leaves behind, and what it does not claim

Two paper archives pegged in the window: what was excluded, and what stayed available but unsaid. The print pack states what the model does not demonstrate: it has no attention, embeddings or training loop and proves nothing about a modern system. It makes the decisions around any model visible.

How it connects to the PD

It carries the AT-LAB approach, making an AI mechanism perceptible, questionable and negotiable for a public, into a library and archive setting that the labs have not worked in. It is not an AT-LAB and is not used as evidence for the four inquiries. Its use for the portfolio is as a new setting for transfer, once it has been delivered and documented.
