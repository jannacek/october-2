The supervision record

The PD is in consolidation. The four-lab structure has not changed since March; the work since then has gone into analysis, writing, dissemination and the next configurations of existing labs.

This Professional Doctorate asks how participatory artistic situations can make AI and data systems perceptible, interpretable and open to situated response — and what kind of knowledge artistic research produces in doing so.

It works through four AT-LABs in health, teaching, civic and artistic settings. Each pairs an artistic design with a light way of reading what happened, and what is learned in one setting shapes the next. Three Boundary Concepts — Concept Clarity, Perceived Agency and Transferability — are used as sensitising lenses for asking comparable questions across settings; they are not a validated measurement scale. The EMI vocabulary is a separate, exploratory strand that helps artist-researchers describe their epistemic contribution inside transdisciplinary teams.

ATMOS_FERA, the 2025 audiovisual performance, is the fourth AT-LAB: an inquiry into how an atmospheric model is translated into spatial sound and image, and what that translation lets an audience perceive.

Formally registered as: Lifestyle and Digital Sovereignty: A new-media arts approach to collective digital empowerment for holistic care

What the evidence supports today

The portfolio separates three levels of claim. This is how the rest of the site is written, and it is the honest shape of a PD in consolidation.

● Demonstrated
  • Four AT-LABs were designed, enacted and documented in four different settings.
  • AT-LAB I produced a 27-response corpus from about 50 participants, now analysed in a secondary analysis.
  • AT-LAB II ran across four course iterations, with questionnaires, student work and reflective dialogue.
  • ATMOS_FERA was performed on 24 October 2025; a paper on it is accepted at ISEA2026.
  • EMI was presented in public for the first time on 23 April 2026, at a session with practitioners from three consortia.
◆ Designed for
  • Other facilitators or teachers re-running a format (the toolkit and prompts are written for this).
  • Boundary Concepts as comparable lenses across very different settings.
  • The AT-LAB cycle as a portable way to structure a situated inquiry.
! Still open
  • Whether any of this produces durable learning, rather than reported insight in the moment.
  • Reciprocity inside HLaS: technical collaboration is documented; changes to other work packages are not.
  • Uptake of the civic toolkit beyond my own workshops.
  • Any validation of EMI as an instrument.

Each lab page repeats this structure for its own case. The Synthesis sets out what the four together support.

Three Contribution Layers

The PD offers three distinct contributions, each at a different level of abstraction above the individual AT-LABs.

Methodology · being consolidated
AT-LAB
A recurring inquiry format built around a participatory artistic design: design → intervention → reading → reflection → feed-forward. It adapts to local conditions, participants and medium, and keeps each case’s conditions visible. A methods manuscript is in preparation.
Read the methodology
Sensitising lenses
Boundary Concepts
Concept Clarity, Perceived Agency and Transferability: lenses for asking comparable questions across settings without collapsing local specificity. They suggest a reading order, clarity → agency → transfer, used as a heuristic and not as a developmental law or a metric.
Read the lenses
Exploratory vocabulary
EMI
Epistemic Mediation Indicators: six dimensions for describing how artistic research contributes to knowledge-making in transdisciplinary collaboration. Developed in HLaS WP2, first presented in public on 23 April 2026, and still exploratory; it is not a validated instrument.
Read the framework
