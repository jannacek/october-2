The AT-LAB is a recurring six-element form, recognised after the fact across four environments. E16 E17 E16 §3.1, §3.4, Table 1 (§4) The six elements, the account of abductive consolidation, and what each lab is allowed to support. The form was recognised retrospectively; no pre-registered design. See the origin note.
The three lenses were named in February 2026, for the Pontevedra talk, and then read back into earlier material. E16 E47 E16 §3.1; E47 slides The date, the first framing as boundary objects and the later move to sensitising lenses. Reading back by the researcher who coined them risks circularity (E16 §3.2).
In the AT-LAB I forms, entanglement is the most common data relation (15 of 27) and articulated control is rare (4). E15 E26 E15 §B (coding table by respondent), §C (tally), §E.1; E16 §5.2 and Figure 1 Each count against the listed respondents; the quote bank (§D) for the wording behind each code. One coder who is also the researcher; a post-hoc lens on forms written for another purpose (E15 §F).
Not knowing the term “digital sovereignty” coexists with high self-rated confidence. E15 E15 §A.1 (Q5, Q6), §C (DC4, Q5), §E.2; the respondent-level table on the AT-LAB I page Of 18 respondents who say they do not know the term, 7 rate their confidence 4 or 5, four of them at 5 (P01, P05, P11, P24). Twenty-seven forms; confidence is a single self-rated item; no claim about prevalence.
At the start of the 2025 course, students’ definitions of AI ranged from algorithmic to metaphorical, so conceptual clarification came first. E18 E38 E18 §1 and its reflection; E16 §4.2, §5.1 The ten definitions grouped by type; the course outline that follows. Ten students, one cohort (E16 §3.4).
The AT-LAB III formats were designed to be re-run by others. E35 E16 E35 card set, prompts, vote screen, activity sheet; E16 §4.3, §5.3 That the kit is documented well enough to be re-run: cards, prompt structure, round sequence. Designed for transfer, not demonstrated: no other facilitator has run it (E16 §5.3, §6).
ATMOS_FERA enacts radiative-transfer data as light, sound and fog conditions. E19 E36 E39 E19 Methodology (translational modelling) and Discussion; E36 pipeline and rider; E39 project page The mapping from data to shader and sound parameters, act by act; the performance documentation. No audience data were collected, and none is claimed. The paper’s Discussion says its claim does not rest on measured responses.
The ISEA2026 paper is accepted; the Forum+ article is accepted for review. E19 E22 E19 acceptance correspondence; E22 editorial email, 1 October 2026 The two statuses as the venues state them. Accepted for presentation and proceedings is not published; accepted for review is not accepted for publication.
The format has not yet been re-run by another facilitator. E16 E16 §3.4, §5.3, §6 The stated limit and what a re-run would test. Named in the manuscript as the format’s next test, not as a requirement of the programme.
