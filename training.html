The rate I use to turn hours into EC

The programme’s documents give two answers. The Study Guide sets 140 hours for 5 EC and 280 hours for the 10 EC of individual training, which is 28 hours per EC. The Graduate Network’s individual-training form divides hours by 24. An earlier version of this page used 24 without saying so. I use the Study Guide’s 28, the stricter of the two, for every figure on this page, and show the form’s 24 beside it for the courses. The choice does not change the result: at 24 the courses count for more, which only widens the margin over the 10-EC maximum.

Collective programme, Graduate Network

The Study Guide sets four 5-EC components, each tied to a learning goal: the seminar series Advances in Artistic and Design Research (five symposia or seminars; Doing artistic and/or design research), the module Ethics of Artistic and Design Research (Acting in a professional manner), the module Understanding Discourses & Practices of Creativity and Innovation (Co-creating innovations) and the module Complex Research Environments (Enabling change). The calculation. The three courses come to 160 h, or 5.71 EC at 28 h per EC. The three conferences come to 2 + 2 + 1 = 5 EC, which is 140 h at the same rate. Together that is 300 h, or 10.71 EC, against a maximum of 10 EC (280 h): the individual elements are complete at the maximum, with 0.71 EC (about 20 h) beyond it. The result does not turn on the rate: at the form’s 24 h per EC the courses alone would be 6.67 EC. Nor does it turn on the conference values being exact: the maximum is reached as long as the three conferences together are worth at least 4.29 EC (120 h). This is my calculation, not a recorded credit.

Teaching

Teaching is allocated 12.5% of PD time. At the Frank Mohr Institute it is also the main site of AT-LAB II, so it is not a separate activity beside the research. The course has run four times:

  • 2023: 1 EC, Discursive Context: AI in Artistic Practice (June).
  • 2024: 2 EC, Artificial Intelligence: In, Within, and Around Your Practice (April–June), across the MADTech, Painting, MAP and IRAP masters.
  • 2025: 2 EC, the third iteration, with a baseline questionnaire at the first class (N = 10).
  • 2026: 1 EC, a design course at MADTech on creative AI workflows, in three sessions.
